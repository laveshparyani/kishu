FAQ

Questions, answered

What details do I need for a reading?

Your date of birth, exact time of birth and place of birth. For kundli matching, I need these details for both people.

I don't know my exact birth time. Can I still get a reading?

Yes. I can do birth time rectification using key events in your life, or use a prashna (horary) chart for specific questions.

Are consultations available online?

Yes. Most consultations happen over video call, phone or WhatsApp, so you can book from anywhere in India or abroad.

Will I have to buy expensive remedies?

No. Remedies are usually mantras, daan, fasting or lifestyle changes. Gemstones are suggested only when your chart clearly supports them, and you're free to buy them anywhere.

Is my information kept private?

Always. Your birth details and questions are used only for your reading and are never shared.
